How I read the market

Five price-action concepts I use on every chart, and teach in every session.

Support & Resistance

Price levels where buyers or sellers have repeatedly stepped in. They frame every trade idea.

Order Block (OB)

The last opposing candle before a strong move, where large orders often sit waiting.

Supply & Demand Zones

Areas where price left quickly, showing an imbalance between sellers and buyers.

Fair Value Gap (FVG)

A three-candle gap that price tends to revisit to fill inefficiency before continuing.

Golden Zone (Fibonacci)

The 0.618–0.786 retracement area, where pullbacks often end and trends resume.
