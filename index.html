🏛️ CORE TRADING METHODOLOGY

THE 5 CORE MARKET STRATEGIES

The foundational concepts I teach in my 1-on-1 sessions. Master how institutional orders drive XAU/USD, US OIL, USTEC, and BTC price action.

01 LIQUIDITY LEVEL

Support & Resistance

Understanding true market turning points versus retail breakout traps.

FALSE BREAKOUT PURGE
◈ Stop Hunts: Retail double tops & bottoms exist to supply liquidity to big players.
◈ S/R Flip: True displacement occurs only after sell-side or buy-side liquidity is swept.
02 INSTITUTIONAL FOOTPRINT

Order Block (OB)

The last opposing candle before aggressive displacement breaks market structure.

MITIGATION RETEST
◈ Final Counter-Candle: The last down-close candle before a violent surge that shatters Market Structure (BOS).
◈ Mitigation Entry: Institutions return price to this exact footprint to close counter-positions in profit.
03 IMBALANCE DYNAMICS

Supply & Demand Zones

Volume delta and order imbalance mapping between discount and premium prices.

PREMIUM // DISCOUNT ARRAY
◈ Origin of Imbalance: Where aggregate limit orders drastically outweigh market execution capacity.
◈ Discount vs Premium: We never buy in premium or sell in discount. We only execute at institutional valuation extremes.
04 PRICE INEFFICIENCY

Fair Value Gap (FVG)

A 3-candle imbalance void created when price expands violently without retesting.

VACUUM REBALANCING
◈ Inefficient Delivery: A void created when candle 1 high and candle 3 low never overlap during heavy expansion.
◈ High R:R Tap: The interbank delivery algorithm relentlessly seeks to re-fill this void before continuing trend.
05 OPTIMAL TRADE ENTRY

Golden Zone (Fibonacci 0.618 - 0.786)

Optimal Trade Entry (OTE) retracement levels where high-confluence setups trigger.

0.618 - 0.786 OTE ZONE
◈ Golden Ratio: The 61.8% to 78.6% retracement zone represents the deepest discount buyers permit before trend expansion.
◈ High Confluence: When the Golden Zone aligns with an Order Block inside an FVG, trade probability is at its highest.
