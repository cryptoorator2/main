Trading Approach

Eight concepts that shape a personal, rule-based approach. The animations are original educational illustrations, not real trades, live signals or guaranteed outcomes.

Support & Resistance

Price often pauses or turns at levels where buyers (support) or sellers (resistance) reacted before.

Order Blocks

A zone around the last opposite candle before a strong move, watched as an area where price may react when revisited.

Fair Value Gaps (FVG)

A three-candle imbalance that leaves a gap between the first and third candle. Price sometimes returns to fill it.

Trendlines

Lines joining higher lows (uptrend) or lower highs (downtrend) to read direction and possible reaction points.

Breakout & Breakdown

Price closing beyond a key resistance (breakout) or support (breakdown), sometimes followed by a retest.

Fibonacci Golden Zone

Retracement levels drawn from a swing; the 50%–61.8% area is the golden zone. Used occasionally, never on its own.

Liquidity Heatmaps

Shows where orders may cluster. This is an illustration only, not real market data.

Supply & Demand Zones

Zones where selling (supply) or buying (demand) pressure previously drove sharp moves.
